# Case study

## Objective
Reduce repetitive conversation handling and produce consistent, reviewable notes for a small business.

## Approach
Start with a short introduction, listen for the business problem, route appropriately, and capture a factual outcome. Keep human review before actions that require connected business systems.

## Builder contribution
Federico designed the business conversation flow, tone, routing expectations, post-call information requirements, and follow-up boundaries, and tested the private voice experience through a browser call.

## Lessons
Natural delivery alone does not make a useful business agent. The workflow must distinguish stated interest, permission, requested actions, and verified completed actions. Structured extraction and actual execution are separate responsibilities.

## Measurement plan
Track time spent reviewing calls, accuracy of extracted fields, interruption responsiveness, routing outcomes, and incorrect booking or sending claims. This repository contains no production KPI results.
